About MessHub

Students often miss what the mess is serving, and feedback gets lost in group chats. MessHub puts the weekly menu, today's meals and a rating form in one place.

What it does

  • Shows today's menu and highlights the meal being served now.
  • Filters the weekly menu by day and meal, with a dish search.
  • Validates and saves meal ratings in the browser (localStorage).
  • Draws a rating chart with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
